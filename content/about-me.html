I am a technology leader with a Ph.D. in Robotics and Human-Machine Interaction from Northwestern University, with experience spanning research, embedded systems, wireless infrastructure, edge AI, and production robotics. My work has consistently focused on building complex hardware-software systems that must operate reliably in the real world.

During my Ph.D., I pioneered wideband tactile rendering systems and developed algorithms for low-signal-to-noise electromechanical devices, with research published in Nature and other leading journals. This work explored how machines can reproduce realistic physical sensations through touch, combining signal processing, control, electromechanical modeling, and human perception.

I am currently a Senior Software Engineer in the robotics group at Axon, where I develop software for a portfolio of deployed robotic and edge-computing devices used in public-safety applications. My work spans embedded and systems software, on-device intelligence, communications, device reliability, and the infrastructure required to bring robotic systems from prototype to real-world deployment.

Outside of my primary role, I continue to work on next-generation human-machine interfaces. I am developing immersive virtual-reality systems that combine haptics, audio, and visual feedback to create more natural and integrated interactions. I am also advancing new haptic touchscreen technologies and developing algorithms for tactile rendering, with the broader goal of making digital interfaces feel more physical, expressive, and intuitive.
